+# BTC Spot-Perpetual Carry Foundation Design
+
+## Objective
+
+Build the accounting, data-quality and multi-leg research contracts required to
+measure a BTC spot-perpetual funding carry. V1 is shadow-only and does not place
+orders or alter the existing directional strategy.
+
+Quarterly futures, naked options and production capital are outside V1.
+
+## Current blockers
+
+- historical replay reports funding separately but does not include funding
+  cashflow in strategy equity and net PnL;
+- BTC spot data is stale and contains duplicate timestamps;
+- basis joins can return the last value indefinitely without a stale flag;
+- funding `mark_price` is mostly unavailable;
+- no dated-futures curve, canonical OI or borrow-cost history exists;
+- the system has no atomic two-leg intent, hedge error or pair episode contract.
+
+The existing `funding_alpha_signal` is a directional contrarian feature. It is
+not a market-neutral carry strategy and cannot serve as the Carry V1 donor.
+
+## Data contract
+
+Every input carries source timestamp, visible-at timestamp, coverage, staleness,
+quality flags and content identity. Price inputs older than one 4h decision
+interval are stale; execution quotes older than one 5m interval are stale.
+Byte-identical duplicate timestamps collapse deterministically, while
+conflicting duplicates fail closed.
+
+The minimum aligned observation contains:
+
+- BTC spot and perpetual executable price proxies at the same cutoff;
+- perpetual mark and index price when available;
+- realized funding settlements and a next-funding estimate only when its source
+  has explicit `published_at` or `visible_at` history;
+- spot-perpetual basis as a diagnostic mark-to-market state;
+- expected funding over a frozen 24h holding horizon;
+- conservative two-leg fees and slippage;
+- spot financing cost and, for reverse carry, borrow rate and availability;
+- margin, maintenance margin and liquidation buffer;
+- instrument multipliers and minimum quantities.
+
+Final realized settlement rates can never be backfilled as earlier forecasts.
+Without a point-in-time next-funding source, expected carry is `UNKNOWN` and no
+candidate episode opens. Missing, stale or unaligned critical data also produces
+`UNKNOWN` and a flat shadow target.
+
+V1 enables only positive carry: long spot and short perpetual. Reverse carry is
+disabled until point-in-time borrow rate and availability history exists. The
+primary financing stress is a pre-registered `10%` annual rate, with `0%`, `5%`
+and `20%` reported as non-promoting sensitivities. Venue fee tier, contract
+multiplier, minimum quantity and margin parameters remain instrument-specific.
+
+## Carry decision contract
+
+An immutable research-only `CarryDecisionSnapshot` records:
+
+- decision and hedge-group identity;
+- confirmed 4h cutoff;
+- long/short instrument identities;
+- aligned source observations and hashes;
+- expected gross funding over 24h and current basis;
+- financing, borrow, fees and slippage;
+- expected net carry and breakeven holding horizon;
+- hedge ratio, maximum hedge error and maximum unhedged duration;
+- margin and liquidation buffers;
+- quality flags and `blocks_order=true` for V1 shadow publication.
+
+Expected net carry equals visible expected funding minus financing, two-leg fees
+and two-leg slippage. Basis is not counted as expected convergence; it enters PnL
+only through actual entry-to-exit mark changes. Breakeven holding time divides
+round-trip costs by visible expected funding per hour and is `UNKNOWN` when that
+rate is non-positive or unavailable.
+
+The snapshot is separate from the directional `DecisionSnapshot` and cannot be
+consumed by the production executor.
+
+## Pair accounting
+
+A pair ledger measures one hedge group:
+
+```text
+spot mark-to-market PnL
++ perpetual mark-to-market PnL
++ funding cashflow
+- spot financing or borrow cost
+- two-leg fees
+- two-leg slippage
+= net carry PnL
+```
+
+Funding enters the versioned carry ledger's cash and equity exactly once at its
+point-in-time settlement. Carry-disabled legacy replay, acceptance summaries and
+goldens remain byte-identical; V1 does not silently rewrite global historical
+accounting.
+The ledger separately reports gross basis PnL, funding, financing, fees,
+slippage, maximum hedge error, unhedged duration, margin usage and liquidation
+distance.
+
+## Execution boundary
+
+The existing V2 target-tracking kernel may later execute each approved leg. A
+new hedge-group coordinator must own leg pairing, partial-fill recovery and
+maximum unhedged exposure. The executor cannot invent the hedge leg or alter the
+pair ratio.
+
+V1 implements no live coordinator. A confirmed 4h cutoff creates only a
+decision; it cannot fill at that same close. Counterfactual legs fill from the
+next visible executable 5m quote using bid/ask, frozen latency and slippage.
+Leg order, partial fills and hedge error are recorded. Exceeding maximum hedge
+error or unhedged duration produces `INVALID_PAIR`, closes no valid carry
+episode and cannot enter PF or return numerators or denominators.
+
+## ETH validation boundary
+
+ETH is not a production expansion in this phase. Before it can validate the
+frozen carry hypothesis, the repository must:
+
+- add current ETH spot and refresh funding/1d data;
+- disable BTC options, COT, on-chain, liquidation and metrics inputs;
+- remove the invalid ETH-to-ETH cross-asset context;
+- run the exact BTC carry formula, thresholds and cost methodology without ETH
+  retuning, while using ETH-specific fee tier, multiplier, minimum quantity,
+  spread/slippage, financing and margin inputs.
+
+ETH results are an independent falsification panel and never feed back into BTC
+threshold selection.
+
+## Acceptance for Carry Foundation
+
+- funding settlement reconciles cash, equity and pair ledger exactly once;
+- basis refuses stale, duplicate or future data;
+- every aligned input is point-in-time reproducible;
+- pair PnL reconciles all legs and costs to floating-point tolerance;
+- missing borrow, financing or margin data fails flat;
+- single-leg fill and hedge-error scenarios are explicitly censored or failed;
+- censored and `INVALID_PAIR` observations are excluded from PF and return Gates
+  and reported with an explicit failure rate;
+- no shadow object reaches strategy, risk or production order subjects;
+- BTC and ETH symbol scopes cannot load each other's auxiliary data;
+- quarterly futures and options trading remain unavailable.
+
+Passing this foundation proves accounting and data-contract correctness only.
+It is not Alpha, OOS or promotion evidence.
