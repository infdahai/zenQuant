+# Trend Participation Donor V1 Implementation Plan
+
+> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.
+
+**Goal:** Build and evaluate an isolated BTC `LONG | FLAT` structural trend donor that must remain below 30% MDD on every fixed non-train path and beat fixed EMA-family and breakout-only controls after realistic costs.
+
+**Architecture:** Keep signal, controls, perturbation, execution, accounting, stages, acceptance, and reporting as separate deterministic research modules. Reuse the audited execution gateway and episode ledger, share only a byte-parity simulated-price quote, and keep target-delta planning private to research so TimeSpace-bound production files remain untouched; do not import the donor into strategy, risk, app, infra, or live adapters. Historical and reused results may reject the donor, while only one precommitted 365-day forward window may make it eligible for a separate activation review.
+
+**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11, frozen dataclasses, pandas/pyarrow, DuckDB-backed Parquet reads, existing `SimulatedGateway`, `PositionEpisodeLedger`, pytest, ruff, canonical JSON/SHA-256.
+
+**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-24-trend-participation-two-layer-design.md`
+
+## Global Constraints
+
+- BTC only; daily spot signal, confirmed 4h decision cutoff, first retained perpetual 5m open strictly after the cutoff.
+- Core state is exactly `FLAT | LONG | UNKNOWN`; it never emits `SHORT`.
+- Entry is prior-55-day high breakout plus EMA20–100 majority; exit is prior-20-day low failure plus loss of EMA majority.
+- At least 100 consecutive valid completed daily rows are required; a real source gap resets warmup and `UNKNOWN` approves flat.
+- T1 is fixed initial capital, fixed 1x entry notional, fixed quantity within an episode, no compounding.
+- T2 scalar is `min(1.50, (0.85 * 0.30) / train_stress_mdd)` and may read only `TRAIN_FULL`.
+- T3 may only reduce exposure; the 28% 5m marked-to-market drawdown kill latches for the remainder of its window.
+- Final T3 MDD must be strictly below 30% on the canonical and all five seed paths in every fixed non-train window.
+- Costs are observed 4bp taker fee plus 1.5bp fallback slippage, with additional 15bp acceptance and 25bp diagnostic turnover stress.
+- Seeds are exactly `42, 123, 456, 789, 1024`; no parameter search or result-driven threshold change.
+- `DEV_2024_REUSED`, `DISCOVERY_2025`, and `TEST_2026_REUSED` may reject but never promote or resize.
+- Forward evidence is exactly `[F, F + 365 calendar days)` with one look; an extension requires a new prospective evidence envelope.
+- EMA controls remain all 81 named members; no best EMA may be selected.
+- OptionsState and TimeSpace remain observation-only and byte-neutral to orders and positions.
+- Local committed manifests, Git SHA, source/code/config hashes, exact commands, and result hashes are sufficient for research; no remote receipt is required.
+- Every task uses TDD for new behavior, preserves the untracked `data` link, and ends in the explicitly listed single-purpose commit or commits.
