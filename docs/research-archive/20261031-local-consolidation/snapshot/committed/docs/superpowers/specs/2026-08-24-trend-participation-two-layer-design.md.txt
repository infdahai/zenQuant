# Trend Participation Two-Layer Design

## Status and product decision

Approved direction as of 2026-08-25. This revision supersedes the original
`z84` long/short donor design in this file.

Build two isolated BTC research layers:

1. `TimeSpace Shadow` measures whether early flips abandon structurally intact
   trends. It remains observation-only.
2. `Trend Participation Donor V1` attempts to earn the persistent BTC trend
   premium with a slow `LONG | FLAT` core. It uses a confirmed structural
   breakout for entry, EMA-family breadth plus market structure for holding,
   and a confirmed structural failure for exit.

The product objective is lexicographic:

```text
first:  the final candidate has MDD < 30% in every fixed OOS window and perturbation path
then:   maximize cost-adjusted OOS net profit and CAGR
then:   prefer stronger worst-window return and persistent-trend capture
```

An MDD of `15%–25%` is the intended risk-use range. `25%–30%` is allowed. An
MDD at or above `30%` is a hard rejection. Very low drawdown is not itself a
success: a path that leaves most of the approved risk budget unused and cannot
beat the simple trend controls is economically rejected.

EMA is not the strategy. EMA20–100 is a control family and one measure of trend
integrity. The donor is a structural state machine. The retired August donor is
not blended into it, and no current risk or execution layer may manufacture a
directional reversal.

## Evidence motivating the revision

The repaired August donor has no adequate directional edge in 2025 late-dev:

- `A1_SIGN` is negative even before costs;
- `A1_SCORE` compresses but does not reverse the loss;
- A5 earns only about `+0.379%` at an average MDD near `1.15%` and becomes
  negative under an additional 15bp;
- its risk layer protects capital but does not create a trend return source.

A strict Binance spot daily reconstruction shows why a slow trend donor is the
right control. With a common EMA100 warmup, `LONG | CASH`, next-session
execution and 8bp one-way costs, EMA20–100 reproduces the reported historical
family result: all 81 variants beat the common-start BTC benchmark, with family
mean return around `+1,967%` versus about `+752%` for buy-and-hold. This history
is diagnostic, not promotion evidence. The important finding is that the edge
survives causal execution and broad parameter variation.

On the exact 2025 fixed-capital comparison, the EMA family also has materially
more gross edge than August, but uses much more drawdown. The design therefore
does not copy an EMA strategy or blindly add leverage. It creates a new donor,
then uses the minimum deterministic risk intervention needed to keep every OOS
path below the approved 30% ceiling.

## Non-goals

- no pure single-EMA strategy and no selection of a best EMA;
- no August parameter reuse, blending, retraining or alias donor;
- no direct `LONG -> SHORT` transition in the core donor;
- no minimum holding-period lock;
- no use of trend age, weekly progress or OptionsState as V1 trading authority;
- no parameter search over lookbacks, thresholds, weights or risk caps;
- no ML, RL, Optuna, learned hazard model or end-to-end black box;
- no GitHub Pro, branch-protection or remote-receipt requirement for local
  research evidence;
- no live activation from a historical or reused result.

## Architecture and authority

```text
confirmed spot daily rows
          |
          +------------------------------+
          |                              |
          v                              v
EMA-family controls              structural donor state
81 named correlated baselines    55d breakout / 20d failure
no donor authority               EMA breadth as hold evidence
          |                              |
          +---------------+--------------+
                          v
             fixed-1x TrendCoreIntent
                          |
                          v
        deterministic risk-budget scalar (after T1 proof)
                          |
                          v
               private research execution
                          |
                          v
        fills / funding / episodes / cost stress

TimeSpace Shadow and OptionsState remain separate observation-only sidecars.
```

TimeSpace Shadow semantics remain governed by
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-23-time-space-shadow-design.md`; this revision
does not silently change its populations, labels or authority. The common
local-evidence and no-live-activation boundaries in this document still apply;
where the older spec requires a remote registration receipt for local research,
this approved umbrella revision supersedes that requirement.

The core donor owns only `LONG | FLAT` research direction. Risk may cap, reduce
or flatten it but may not create `SHORT`. Execution may track the approved
target but may not score the market, add exposure outside the target or reverse
the position.

A future tactical-short sleeve is a separate donor and a separate approval.
Its aggregate ex-ante risk allocation may not exceed 25% of the total strategy
risk budget, and it may act only while the trend core is `FLAT` **and** hard
safety permits new risk. In addition, filled core position must be zero within
quantity epsilon and no core order may remain open. Hard safety may reduce
existing exposure but may never create a tactical short. On a future
`FLAT -> LONG` core transition, any tactical short must first cancel its entry
orders, flatten reduce-only, and confirm zero filled position before the core
may open long. This document defines that interface boundary but does not
define or implement a short signal. `UNKNOWN` and a safety-flattened exposure
whose latent core remains `LONG` never authorize the short sleeve.

Strategy, risk, execution, app, infra, ingestors and live adapters must not
import this research donor. V1 runs through an isolated research adapter.
Shadow/control OFF and ON parity replays the same frozen real August/production
event stream through the operational path, once without the sidecars and once
with them loaded observation-only. It compares snapshots, targets, orders,
fills, equity and episodes byte-for-byte. Empty streams, empty objects, mocks
without actual operational decisions, or comparing one object to its own copy
cannot satisfy this contract.

## Phase 0: repair and freeze the daily source

The raw Binance spot archive currently reconstructs into a continuous UTC
daily series from `2017-08-17` through `2026-08-23`, with 3,294 unique rows.
The checked-in conversion path is not authoritative: the no-header CSV reader
uses `skiprows=1`, drops the first day of monthly files, and produced a stale
3,045-row parquet ending `2026-03-31`.

Before computing any new donor result:

1. add a failing regression test for no-header Binance CSV conversion;
2. repair the converter without changing unrelated ingestion semantics;
3. refresh the raw archive through the latest fully completed UTC daily close,
   then rebuild the BTCUSDT spot 1d parquet;
4. require a unique, monotonic, gap-free UTC daily grid and exact OHLC
   invariants;
5. freeze the ordered raw-file manifest hash, normalization-code hash,
   normalized content hash, first/last completed cutoff and row count;
6. keep any day later than the last fully completed UTC daily close out of the
   frozen corpus.

Missing, duplicate, conflicting, non-finite or non-positive rows yield
`INSUFFICIENT_DATA`. Perpetual candles may not proxy spot. Source repair is
blind data engineering: it may not calculate a donor result or change the
formula after results are visible.

## Control baselines

Every experiment reports three executable controls on the exact same capital,
window, perpetual execution prices, funding and cost assumptions:

1. `PERP_LONG_1X`: long BTC perpetual for the complete evaluation window;
2. `EMA_FAMILY_20_100`: 81 separate `close > EMA_n` long/cash paths for
   `n = 20..100`, with `adjust=false`, `min_periods=n` and no selected winner;
3. `BREAKOUT_ONLY_55_20`: the structural entry and exit below without EMA
   breadth confirmation.

Spot buy-and-hold is reported separately as `SPOT_BUY_AND_HOLD_REFERENCE`. It
explains the underlying asset drift but is not an executable gate comparator
because it has neither perpetual fills nor funding.

All executable paths use the same eligibility and initialization rules. Indicators may
warm on valid point-in-time history before an evaluation window, but PnL starts
from flat at the first eligible 4h cutoff inside that window. `PERP_LONG_1X`
enters at that path's first execution event. A full-history path first becomes
eligible after 100 consecutive valid daily rows. This common start is recorded
explicitly; no path may gain performance from a hidden earlier start or a
carried-in position.

`BREAKOUT_ONLY_55_20` is an exact long/flat state machine:

```text
FLAT -> LONG when close_t > max(high[t-55:t])
LONG -> FLAT when close_t < min(low[t-20:t])
```

It has no EMA condition. All three controls are reported at fixed 1x. After T2
freezes its scalar, the same constant scalar is also applied independently to
each control so that final-stage economics can be compared at the same ex-ante
risk scale without using OOS results to resize either side.

The EMA family is summarized by its median, 25th/75th percentiles, complete
range and all 81 named results. Family members are correlated controls, not 81
independent statistical samples. No best member may become the donor.

## Trend Participation Donor V1

### Point-in-time daily inputs

For a UTC daily bar `[D, D+1 day)`, every OHLC field is available at
`D+1 day`. It may first influence the first confirmed 4h decision cutoff
strictly later than that timestamp. A bar available at 00:00 UTC therefore
first affects the 04:00 UTC decision, never the 00:00 decision.

At daily index `t`, compute from completed rows only:

```text
entry_boundary_t = max(high[t-55 : t])     # previous 55 daily bars
exit_boundary_t  = min(low[t-20 : t])      # previous 20 daily bars

ema_breadth_t = count(close_t > EMA_n_t for n in 20..100) / 81
ema_majority_t = ema_breadth_t >= 0.50
```

The `55/20` horizons are the frozen canonical Turtle-style structural control,
not parameters selected from BTC results. The `0.50` breadth boundary is the
literal family majority. Changing any horizon, family member or threshold
creates a new donor ID and requires a new design approval.

At least 100 consecutive completed daily bars are required. A real source gap
resets warmup. Invalid input produces `UNKNOWN`, whose approved research target
is flat.

### Mutually exclusive core state machine

The core state is exactly `FLAT | LONG | UNKNOWN`:

```text
FLAT:
  if close_t > entry_boundary_t AND ema_majority_t:
      LONG
  else:
      FLAT

LONG:
  if close_t < exit_boundary_t AND NOT ema_majority_t:
      FLAT
  else:
      LONG

UNKNOWN:
  target = FLAT
  after 100 new consecutive valid rows, resume from FLAT
```

Entry is caused by a confirmed structural breakout, not by EMA alone. Exit
requires both a structural failure and loss of broad trend support. The two
conditions provide hysteresis without a time lock. A large price increase,
overbought reading, incomplete first weekly bar or short-term reversal cannot
independently exit or reverse the core.

The state machine never emits `SHORT`. A completed `LONG -> FLAT` transition
must reach flat before any separately approved tactical sleeve could act.

### Diagnostic time-space fields

The donor records, without granting authority:

- days and retained 4h bars since structural entry;
- breakout age and current UTC-week completion fraction;
- EMA breadth and its 1/3/7-day changes;
- distance to entry and exit boundaries;
- prior visible swing integrity;
- path efficiency, episode MFE/MAE and drawdown from the favorable close;
- TimeSpace and OptionsState IDs available at the same cutoff.

These fields explain whether early exits or later structural failures drive
performance. They may not change V1 entry, exit, size or safety.

## Research stages and risk use

The experiment is sequential and each stage preserves the previous result:

```text
C0  controls only: PERP_LONG_1X, all EMA family members, breakout-only
T1  structural donor, fixed initial capital, fixed 1x, no compounding
T2  T1 plus one deterministic risk-budget scalar
T3  T2 plus existing hard safety and the fixed 28% research drawdown kill
```

No A2–A5 regime multiplier, confidence sizing, no-trade band or cost gate may
enter T1. This answers whether the donor itself makes money.

T2 uses `TRAIN_FULL` evidence only to compute one scalar, then freezes it for
every non-train window and perturbation path:

```text
train_stress_mdd = maximum T1 MDD across canonical TRAIN_FULL and its five seed paths
risk_scalar = min(1.50, (0.85 * 0.30) / train_stress_mdd)
```

MDD is represented as a positive equity-loss fraction in `[0, 1]`. If
`train_stress_mdd <= 0`, the result is invalid. The `0.85` factor reserves a 15%
buffer below the 30% product ceiling; it is not tuned. T2 multiplies the core
fixed-notional target by this constant scalar. It does not use OOS results,
current drawdown or a regime label to resize retrospectively.

T3 may cancel, reduce or flatten for existing `KILL`, `UNSAFE`,
`REDUCE_ONLY` and mechanical cap violations. It cannot add exposure, restore a
position before the next confirmed 4h decision or change direction. A research
drawdown kill latches when marked-to-market research equity first reaches 28%
drawdown from its path high-water mark. It cancels open orders, submits only
reduce-only flattening orders, and keeps approved exposure at zero for the
remainder of that evaluation path. It does not mutate the latent core state or
turn safety-flat exposure into tactical-short eligibility. This fixed latch
leaves execution room below the 30% absolute acceptance ceiling; a gap through
30% still fails the path.

Risk-budget utilization is computed per T3 window/path acceptance row. A row is
`UNDERUSED_RISK_BUDGET` only when its MDD is below 10% and its net CAGR trails
the same-scalar EMA-family median for that exact window and path. The final
candidate fails underuse when the canonical `FORWARD_365D` row or at least 4/5
forward seed rows carry that flag. Historical underuse flags remain diagnostic.
The remaining bands are also row-local:

- 10%–15% is conservative;
- 15%–25% is intended;
- 25%–30% is allowed stress use;
- at or above 30% is hard rejection.

## Execution and accounting

T1–T3 use fixed initial capital and no compounding for causal attribution. A
secondary compounded equity curve may be reported but cannot select or promote
the donor.

`fixed 1x` means one entry notional equal to initial capital. At a
`FLAT -> LONG` fill, quantity is fixed from that entry's execution price and is
held unchanged until the core exits; it is not rebalanced every 4h as price
moves. T2 multiplies that entry quantity by its one frozen scalar. `PERP_LONG_1X`
uses the same one-time quantity construction, so it does not receive a
different constant-leverage rebalance assumption.

Equity is marked at every retained perpetual 5m event and at the final event in
the half-open window. MDD uses the initial equity plus this complete 5m
marked-to-market series. An open terminal position is not synthetically filled:
its unrealized PnL is included at the last mark, while the episode remains open
and is excluded from closed-episode PF. Funding and fees are booked at their
actual event cutoffs. If equity is non-positive, the path is `RUIN`, MDD is
`100%`, CAGR is invalid and the path fails. Otherwise:

```text
net_cagr = (terminal_equity / initial_equity) ** (365.2425 / elapsed_days) - 1
```

The first execution event is the first retained BTCUSDT perpetual 5m bar open
strictly after the confirmed 4h decision cutoff. The adapter stores the signal
cutoff and execution event timestamp separately. It reuses audited fill,
fee/slippage, funding and episode-accounting primitives in a private research
namespace; it does not publish production subjects or create production
`DecisionSnapshot` records.

Each path reports actual realized turnover and reconciles:

```text
gross_trading_pnl = realized_fill_pnl + terminal_unrealized_pnl
net_pnl_base = gross_trading_pnl + funding - fees
net_pnl_15bp = net_pnl_base - realized_turnover_notional * 0.0015
net_pnl_25bp = net_pnl_base - realized_turnover_notional * 0.0025
```

Slippage already embedded in actual fill prices is not deducted twice. The
15bp result is an acceptance input; 25bp is a non-promoting stress diagnostic.

Closed-episode PF uses episode net PnL after fees and funding. Open episodes are
reported separately and excluded. Its exact boundary rules are:

- no closed episode: `INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE` and Gate failure;
- positive episodes and no loss episodes: `NO_LOSS_EPISODES`; pass only with
  at least two closed episodes;
- no positive episode: PF is `0`;
- otherwise: sum of positive episode net PnL divided by the absolute sum of
  negative episode net PnL.

`switches/day` counts filled-position side transitions attributed to the UTC
day of the zero-crossing fill. Entry and exit between long and flat are reported
as turnover actions but are not direction switches; the V1 core therefore must
have zero long/short switches. For any future sleeve, Gate uses the maximum UTC
daily count, while the mean remains diagnostic:

- `<=1/day`: no penalty;
- `(1,2]/day`: soft flag, may enter dev;
- `>2/day`: normal Gate rejection;
- `>5/day`: independent absolute rejection.

Use the repository's authoritative half-open temporal split and seeds
`42, 123, 456, 789, 1024`:

| Window ID | Exact UTC half-open interval | Warmup | PnL reset | T2 scalar | May reject | May promote |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `TRAIN_FULL` | `[2020-01-01, 2024-01-01)` | PIT pre-start | flat | yes | integrity only | no |
| `TRAIN_YEAR_2020..2023` | each UTC calendar year inside `TRAIN_FULL` | PIT pre-start | flat per slice | no | no | no |
| `DEV_2024_REUSED` | `[2024-01-01, 2025-01-01)` | PIT pre-start | flat | no | yes | no |
| `DISCOVERY_2025` | `[2025-01-01, 2026-01-01)` | PIT pre-start | flat | no | yes | no |
| `TEST_2026_REUSED` | `[2026-01-01, F)` | PIT pre-start | flat | no | yes | no |
| `FORWARD_365D` | `[F, F + 365 calendar days)` | PIT pre-start | flat | no | yes | yes |

`F` is the exact first confirmed 4h cutoff strictly after the common frozen
source cutoff. It is written as nanoseconds into the committed execution
manifest before any `FORWARD_365D` market event is read. The complete table,
including resolved timestamps, source coverage and role flags, is hash-bound
into every result.

No result may pool rows across evidence roles. `TRAIN_FULL` is the only domain allowed to produce
the T2 scalar. Annual train slices diagnose regime dependence but cannot alter
it. Dev, discovery, reused test and forward rows may reject the identity but
may never resize it. Any implementation that calls 2020–2023 OOS, omits 2024,
or lets a partial 2026 result enter training fails the split contract.

Local evidence identity consists of committed formula/config manifests, Git
commit SHA, ordered source-file and normalized-content hashes, exact commands,
environment lockfile hash and immutable result hashes. No GitHub Pro,
self-hosted runner, branch-protection rule or remote provider receipt is
required for research, rejection or continued observation.

Local evidence cannot authorize paper/live capital. Any later deployment review
may define a separate operational trust contract without changing the donor
formula or relabeling reused history.

The five deterministic perturbation paths reuse
`zenquant.historical.perturbation.perturb_events` with an execution-manifest
hash of that module and this exact config:

```text
seeds = [42, 123, 456, 789, 1024]
price_noise_pct = 0.001       # independent Gaussian factor per OHLC field
volume_noise_pct = 0.05       # independent Gaussian factor, floored at zero
bar_dropout_pct = 0.02        # independent Bernoulli drop
execution_delay_bars = 0
spread_noise_bps = 0
all adversarial modes = disabled
```

For each seed, a fresh `random.Random(seed)` is applied independently to each
ordered stream. Spot daily receives price and volume noise but no dropout, so
an injected robustness path cannot masquerade as a real required-source gap.
Copied perpetual 4h decision events and 5m execution events receive the complete
noise and dropout config. Funding events and the canonical month-label set are
unchanged. OHLC repair is exact: `high = max(noisy_high, noisy_open,
noisy_close)` and `low = min(noisy_low, noisy_open, noisy_close)`. Streams do
not share RNG state or synchronized drop masks. Source integrity runs before
perturbation, and every perturbed row carries its base row ID and seed.

Any change to the kernel hash, stream list, RNG construction, draw order,
amplitude or repair rule creates a new experiment identity. Perturbations
measure path stability, not five independent samples.

### Outcome-independent persistent-trend labels

Trend-capture gates use consecutive, non-overlapping UTC calendar months. A
month is labeled from spot prices alone, without donor positions or PnL:

```text
sigma84 = standard deviation of the 84 daily log returns visible before month start
monthly_move = log(last_completed_daily_close / first_completed_daily_open)
path_efficiency = abs(monthly_move) / sum(abs(daily_log_return_within_month))

persistent = abs(monthly_move) >= sigma84 * sqrt(completed_days)
             AND path_efficiency >= 0.50
direction = UP if monthly_move > 0 else DOWN
```

The daily open and closes used for labeling come from the same frozen spot
corpus. Donor and `PERP_LONG_1X` PnL on those labeled months use their own
causal perpetual fills. Labels are computed once from the canonical
unperturbed spot corpus, frozen by hash, and reused unchanged for all seed
paths. A month without 84 valid prior returns is `UNLABELED`.

Monthly strategy PnL is the change in 5m marked-to-market perpetual equity
between the first retained event at or after consecutive UTC month boundaries.
This slices a multi-month open episode rather than assigning its whole profit
to the exit month; fills, fees and funding remain assigned at their actual
event cutoffs. Capture is the ratio of aggregate PnL sums across labeled months,
never an average of per-month ratios. These labels are evaluation-only and may
not affect donor state or size.

```text
up_capture_15 = sum(T3 net_pnl_15bp in persistent-up months)
                / sum(PERP_LONG_1X gross_pnl in the same months)

t3_down_loss_15 = max(0, -sum(T3 net_pnl_15bp in persistent-down months))
perp_down_loss_15 = max(0, -sum(PERP_LONG_1X net_pnl_15bp in the same months))
```

A non-positive uptrend denominator or a zero `perp_down_loss_15` is
`INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE` for its respective comparative gate, not an automatic
pass.

There is exactly one forward look for this experiment, at `F + 365 calendar
days`. Sufficient future evidence at that precommitted endpoint means at least
five closed canonical core episodes and at least one persistent-up and one
persistent-down month. A path with fewer than two closed episodes is
`INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE` for episode PF. If any aggregate sufficiency condition is
missing at the fixed endpoint, the result is `INSUFFICIENT_FORWARD_SAMPLE` and
cannot promote. The endpoint may not slide until the first profitable or
sufficient observation. Any extension requires a separately approved
prospective evidence envelope frozen before reading events after the old
endpoint.

## Profit-first gates

Historical and reused evidence can only reject or continue observation. A donor
may reach `ELIGIBLE_FOR_SEPARATE_ACTIVATION_REVIEW` only with sufficient future
evidence and all of the following. Unless a stage is named explicitly, gates
apply to the final T3 candidate. `canonical` means the unperturbed path inside
one evidence role; seed-path results are never pooled with it. Historical role
metrics are compared as role-level values, while forward gates use only
`FORWARD_365D` rows. Historical profit may never offset a forward loss.

Every acceptance row records `stage_id`, `path_id` (`canonical` or seed),
`window_id`, evidence role, metric name, reducer, numerator, denominator,
threshold, sample count, insufficient-sample policy, result and input hashes.
The final verdict is a pure reduction over these rows; report code may not infer
a missing scope or substitute an aggregate.

### Hard safety and integrity

- T3 MDD is strictly below 30% in every fixed non-train window, on the
  canonical path and all five seed paths;
- executor-created exposure, unexpected reversal and target overshoot are zero;
- source, accounting, episode and parity checks reconcile exactly;
- normal and absolute switch ceilings pass;
- T3 never creates `SHORT` core exposure.

### Hard economics

- In every non-train historical role containing at least 180 elapsed days, T1
  fixed-1x `net_pnl_15bp` is positive canonically and in at least 4/5 seed
  paths. A shorter role is `INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE`, not a pass.
- Across those historical roles, the median of T1 fixed-1x role-level
  `net_cagr_15bp` is not below the median of the corresponding fixed-1x
  EMA-family medians.
- In every sufficiently sampled historical role, T1 fixed-1x
  `net_cagr_15bp` is not below `BREAKOUT_ONLY_55_20` fixed-1x.
- On `FORWARD_365D` alone, T1 fixed-1x `net_pnl_15bp` is positive
  canonically and in at least 4/5 seed paths.
- On `FORWARD_365D` alone, T1 fixed-1x `net_cagr_15bp` is not below
  `BREAKOUT_ONLY_55_20` fixed-1x on the canonical path and at least 4/5 seed
  paths.
- On `FORWARD_365D` alone, T3 `net_pnl_15bp` is positive canonically and in at
  least 4/5 seed paths. `RUIN` and `INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE` are failures.
- On `FORWARD_365D` alone, T3 `net_cagr_15bp` is not below the median
  EMA-family member after every member receives the same frozen T2 scalar, on
  the canonical path and at least 4/5 seed paths.
- On `FORWARD_365D` alone, T3 `net_cagr_15bp` is not below
  `BREAKOUT_ONLY_55_20` after the control receives the same frozen T2 scalar,
  on the canonical path and at least 4/5 seed paths.
- On `FORWARD_365D` alone, T3 persistent-uptrend net capture is at
  least 70% of `PERP_LONG_1X` gross profit on the same labeled months.
- On `FORWARD_365D` alone, T3 persistent-downtrend loss is smaller than
  `PERP_LONG_1X` loss.
- T3 closed-episode PF is at least 1.0 on every sufficiently sampled forward
  path.

Calendar-year and episode PnL concentration are reported as diagnostics, not
hard gates: a one-year forward sample cannot satisfy an honest multi-year
concentration test.

### Overlay retention

- T2 must reconcile exactly to T1 direction and the frozen risk scalar; every
  T3 deviation must be exposure-reducing and attributed to a named hard-safety
  event;
- For every non-train window/path row with positive T2 `net_pnl_15bp`, T3 must
  retain at least 80% of that row's T2 `net_pnl_15bp`. The exception applies
  only when that same T2 row has MDD at or above 30%; it never spills into
  another row and never waives T3 forward-profit or MDD gates.
- A candidate meeting the forward `UNDERUSED_RISK_BUDGET` failure reducer is
  rejected even if its Sharpe or MDD improves.

When roles differ in length, historical comparison uses annualized net CAGR.
The primary forward comparison is full-forward net CAGR after 15bp; tie-breakers
are worst historical role return, persistent-trend capture and lower turnover,
in that order. Sharpe and raw hit rate are diagnostics, not primary objectives.

## Rejection conditions

Reject this donor identity rather than tune it when:

- T1 fixed-1x economics are negative after 15bp;
- edge exists only after selecting an EMA, year, seed, regime or age bucket;
- entry works only with same-close execution or another lookahead assumption;
- profit requires breaching 30% MDD in any fixed non-train path;
- T2/T3 destroys trend profit merely to improve Sharpe or produce very low MDD;
- the donor cannot match the EMA-family median after risk and cost matching;
- EMA breadth cannot match the breakout-only control at the required stages;
- missing spot data requires perpetual or carried-value fallback;
- any changed horizon, threshold, authority field or exit is presented under
  the same donor ID.
